New to Canada, or Leaving It

The year you arrive and the year you leave are not ordinary tax years. You're resident for only part of the year, some credits shrink to the days you lived here, and pensions, savings and investments from home are often taxed differently in Canada than you'd expect.

Plenty of people arriving from the UK and Europe have never filed a tax return in their lives, and Australians are used to a very different system. We prepare the Canadian return and work alongside your adviser at home.

Straight Answers

Can we buy HR without moving our accounting to you?

Yes, and plenty of clients do. The HR plans are complete on their own and we'll work alongside your accountant or payroll provider. The same is true in reverse — take the books and keep your HR wherever it sits today.

We're five people. Aren't we too small for an HR plan?

Small businesses are where employment mistakes cost the most, because nobody in the building has catching them as their job. HR Essentials exists for exactly this size. If a monthly plan is more than you need, buy the one-off work instead — a contract, a handbook, a departure handled properly.

Can't software do all this now?

Software does the typing, and we use good software — it's why these prices work. It doesn't decide whether your contractor is really an employee, notice that your WorkSafeBC classification is wrong, or sit with you when you have to let someone go. You're paying for the judgement.

Why pay you when tax software is free?

For a genuinely simple return, free software does the job, and we'll tell you if that's you. You're paying for the returns software can't think through: the year you moved to Canada or left it, a rental, a business, or income from more than one country.

I've just moved to Canada. Do I need to file?

In most cases, yes. If you became resident partway through the year you report your income from the day you arrived. Filing is also how benefits such as the GST/HST credit start, and how your RRSP room begins to build. We'll confirm what applies to you on the first call.

Can you do my UK, Australian or US return too?

No. We prepare the Canadian return only. We'll give your adviser at home what they need from our side, and point you to one if you don't have one.

Do you replace our accountant?

Usually yes on Books or above — your year-end statements and corporate tax return are included. If you'd rather keep your accountant, we hand them a clean year-end package and take that portion out of your monthly price.

What happens if you get something wrong?

We carry professional liability insurance, and we tell you the moment we find a mistake, including our own. If a filing is late because of us, we pay the penalty and the interest and we don't invoice for the fix.

Is our information safe?

Your files stay in Canada. Two-factor sign-in on every system, no personal information sent by plain email, and a confidentiality agreement signed before we see anything.
